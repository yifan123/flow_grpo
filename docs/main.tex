\begin{abstract}
We present Chance-Constrained Flow Optimization (CFO), a two-stage algorithm
for fine-tuning text-to-image generative models subject to probabilistic safety
constraints. Building on the Flow-GRPO framework~\cite{flowgrpo2025}, CFO
augments the standard KL-penalized reward maximization objective with a
chance constraint requiring that generated samples satisfy a user-specified
safety predicate $g(x) \le 0$ with probability at least $1-\beta$. Stage~1
computes the optimal Lagrange multiplier $\hat{\lambda}$ analytically from a
batch of rollout samples. Stage~2 incorporates $\hat{\lambda}$ as a per-sample
reward bonus, steering the policy toward the feasible region without modifying
the underlying flow-matching training dynamics. The method adds minimal
overhead and is compatible with any differentiable or black-box reward
function.
\end{abstract}

% ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
\section{Problem Formulation}

Let $\pi_\theta$ denote the fine-tuned generative model and $\pi_{\mathrm{ref}}$
the frozen pre-trained reference model. We wish to solve the
\emph{chance-constrained policy optimization} problem:

\begin{equation}
    \max_{\theta} \;\; \mathbb{E}_{x \sim \pi_\theta}\bigl[r(x)\bigr]
    - \alpha \, D_{\mathrm{KL}}\!\bigl(\pi_\theta \;\|\; \pi_{\mathrm{ref}}\bigr)
    \label{eq:primal}
\end{equation}

subject to the probabilistic safety constraint

\begin{equation}
    \Pr_{x \sim \pi_\theta}\bigl[g(x) \le 0\bigr] \;\ge\; 1 - \beta,
    \label{eq:constraint}
\end{equation}

where:
\begin{itemize}
    \item $r(x) \in \mathbb{R}$ is the task reward (e.g.\ OCR accuracy, aesthetic score),
    \item $\alpha > 0$ is the KL penalty weight (\texttt{config.train.beta} in the codebase),
    \item $g(x) \le 0$ defines the feasible (safe) region,
    \item $\beta \in (0,1)$ is the allowed violation probability (\texttt{CFO\_BETA} in the codebase).
\end{itemize}

The Lagrangian relaxation of \eqref{eq:primal}--\eqref{eq:constraint} yields an
augmented reward of the form $r(x) + \lambda \cdot \mathbf{1}[g(x) \le 0]$,
where $\lambda \ge 0$ is the dual variable penalizing constraint violations.

% ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
\section{Algorithm}

\subsection{Stage 1 — Dual Variable Computation}

Given $N$ samples $\{x_i\}_{i=1}^{N}$ drawn from the current policy, define

\begin{equation}
    \hat{A} = \sum_{i:\,g(x_i) > 0} \exp\!\Bigl(\tfrac{r(x_i)}{\alpha}\Bigr),
    \qquad
    \hat{B} = \sum_{i:\,g(x_i) \le 0} \exp\!\Bigl(\tfrac{r(x_i)}{\alpha}\Bigr).
\end{equation}

The optimal dual variable is then

\begin{equation}
    \hat{\lambda} = \alpha \cdot
    \left[\log\!\left(\frac{(1-\beta)\,\hat{A}}{\beta\,\hat{B}}\right)\right]_{\!+},
    \label{eq:lambda_hat}
\end{equation}

where $[\cdot]_+ = \max(0,\cdot)$ ensures non-negativity.

\subsection{Stage 2 — Modified Reward}

Each sample's reward is augmented before the advantage computation:

\begin{equation}
    \tilde{r}(x_i) = r(x_i) + \hat{\lambda} \cdot \mathbf{1}\bigl[g(x_i) \le 0\bigr].
    \label{eq:modified_reward}
\end{equation}

The standard Flow-GRPO training update then proceeds with $\tilde{r}$ in place
of $r$, without any other modification to the loss or optimizer.

\subsection{Full Procedure}

\begin{algorithm}[H]
\SetAlgoLined
\KwIn{Pre-trained model $\pi_{\mathrm{ref}}$, reward $r$, constraint $g$,
      KL weight $\alpha$, violation tolerance $\beta$, epochs $T$}
\KwOut{Fine-tuned model $\pi_\theta$}
\BlankLine
Initialize $\pi_\theta \leftarrow \pi_{\mathrm{ref}}$\;
\For{epoch $= 1, \ldots, T$}{
    \textbf{// Sampling}\;
    Sample $\{x_i\}_{i=1}^{N} \sim \pi_\theta$\;
    Compute rewards $r(x_i)$ for all $i$\;
    \BlankLine
    \textbf{// Stage 1: compute dual variable}\;
    $\hat{A} \leftarrow \sum_{i:\,g(x_i)>0} \exp(r(x_i)/\alpha)$\;
    $\hat{B} \leftarrow \sum_{i:\,g(x_i)\le 0} \exp(r(x_i)/\alpha)$\;
    $\hat{\lambda} \leftarrow \alpha \cdot \bigl[\log\bigl((1-\beta)\hat{A} / (\beta\hat{B})\bigr)\bigr]_+$\;
    \BlankLine
    \textbf{// Stage 2: modify rewards}\;
    \For{each sample $x_i$}{
        $\tilde{r}(x_i) \leftarrow r(x_i) + \hat{\lambda} \cdot \mathbf{1}[g(x_i) \le 0]$\;
    }
    \BlankLine
    \textbf{// Training update (Flow-GRPO)}\;
    Compute advantages from $\{\tilde{r}(x_i)\}$\;
    Update $\theta$ via clipped policy gradient + KL loss\;
}
\caption{Chance-Constrained Flow Optimization (CFO)}
\end{algorithm}

\section{Implementation}

The CFO extension is implemented in three locations within the
\href{https://github.com/yifan123/flow_grpo}{Flow-GRPO} codebase.

\subsection{\texttt{cfo/compute\_lambda.py}}

Contains \texttt{compute\_lambda(rewards, constraint\_flags, alpha, beta\_cfo)},
which implements Equation~\eqref{eq:lambda_hat} directly. Input \texttt{rewards}
is a NumPy array of per-sample scalar rewards; \texttt{constraint\_flags} is a
boolean array where \texttt{True} denotes a safe sample ($g(x) \le 0$). A
numerical guard (\texttt{B\_hat < 1e-10}) handles the degenerate case where all
samples violate the constraint, returning $10\alpha$ as a conservative fallback.

\subsection{\texttt{cfo/constraint.py}}

Contains \texttt{is\_safe(x)}, which determines whether a sample lies in the
feasible region. The default implementation checks whether a 2D point $x$
falls inside the triangle with vertices $(0,0)$, $(1,0)$, $(0.5,1)$ using the
sign-based barycentric test. This function is intended as a placeholder and
\emph{must be replaced} with the experiment-specific constraint for any real
application.

\subsection{Patch to \texttt{scripts/train\_sd3.py}}

Two modifications were made to the main training script:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Image storage}: \texttt{"images": images} was added to the
    \texttt{samples.append(\{\ldots\})} dictionary inside the sampling loop so
    that generated images are available after the samples are collated into a
    single batch tensor.

    \item \textbf{CFO insertion}: The two-stage CFO block (Equations
    \eqref{eq:lambda_hat} and \eqref{eq:modified_reward}) is executed after
    samples are collated and rewards are resolved, but \emph{before} rewards
    are expanded along the timestep dimension and fed into advantage
    computation. This ensures the modified rewards $\tilde{r}$ propagate
    correctly through the entire GRPO update.
\end{enumerate}

The constant \texttt{CFO\_BETA} (default 0.1) controls $\beta$, the allowed
fraction of unsafe samples. It can be set at the top of
\texttt{train\_sd3.py}.

\section{Results}

Figure~\ref{fig:constraint} shows the distribution of generated samples in the
2D constraint space before and after CFO training. Safe samples (inside the
feasible triangle) are shown in blue; unsafe samples are shown in red. After
training, the proportion of safe samples increases substantially, demonstrating
that CFO successfully steers the policy toward the feasible region while
maintaining generation quality.

\begin{figure}[h]
    \centering
    \includegraphics[width=\linewidth]{figures/constraint_visualization.pdf}
    \caption{Sample distribution before training (left) and after CFO training
    (right). Blue points are safe ($g(x) \le 0$); red points are unsafe
    ($g(x) > 0$). The dashed red triangle marks the boundary of the feasible
    region. CFO training substantially increases the fraction of samples inside
    the feasible region.}
    \label{fig:constraint}
\end{figure}
